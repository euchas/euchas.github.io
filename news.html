AUG 2026

Joining Cambridge as the first LCLU Senior Fellow

I've joined the Leverhulme Centre for Life in the Universe at the University of Cambridge as its first Senior Fellow, working in Nick Tosca's lab. My work there asks whether features that look like possible signs of life on Mars could instead be made by purely geological and chemical processes, and what that tells us about early Mars and early Earth.

FEB 2025

Why is Mars red?

In Nature Communications, we showed that the planet's red dust owes its colour to ferrihydrite, a mineral that forms in cool water, pointing to an early Mars that was wetter and colder than previously thought.
